VIDEO GAME SALES BY GENRE AND REGION

Presented by Asuna Dai and Umurerwa Kalisa Tania

The aim of this visualization is to represent video game sales with an interactive treemap. Video games are grouped by genre, allowing users to compare sales across different categories. This page is the default view where each hexagon represents one genre. You may select a genre(s), and use the 'View Genre(s)' button to navigate to the treemap view, to examine the corresponding sales performance.
